Executive Summary

The Big Idea

📋 Core Concept

Stok is a mobile-first inventory management application that enables shop owners, retailers, and small merchants to track stock, record sales, analyze business performance, and manage multiple shops — all from their smartphone.

💡 The Problem

Traditional POS systems cost $1,000-5,000, require extensive training, and are complex. Small businesses need an affordable, intuitive solution.

✨ The Solution

Zero hardware cost — Works on any smartphone. 5-minute setup — Anyone can use it. Full offline mode — Works without internet, syncs when online. Affordable — Freemium model with paid tiers.
