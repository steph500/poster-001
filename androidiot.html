Executive Summary

The Big Idea

📋 Core Concept

Android IoT transforms everyday smartphones into intelligent IoT devices for motion detection, environmental monitoring, and remote control — all without expensive hardware.

💡 The Problem

Traditional IoT solutions cost $100-500, require specialized hardware, and are complex to set up. Most people already have powerful smartphones sitting idle.

✨ Our Solution

Zero hardware cost — Use devices you already own. Multi-purpose — Motion, sensors, control in one app. Instant setup — Download and go in 30 seconds. Portable — Monitoring anywhere.
